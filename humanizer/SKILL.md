# Humanizer

Rewrite the supplied text without changing its facts, intent, audience, or
required format. Remove canned transitions, repetitive summaries, inflated
claims, generic praise, fake quotations, and mechanical section symmetry.

Prefer concrete language, varied sentence rhythm, and the author's apparent
voice. Return the revised text directly unless the caller requests a file.
